Why is sharing simple things so complicated?

You create something visual — a dashboard, a report, a prototype, a slide deck. Now you need to share it. Suddenly you're facing:

“Create an account”

Every service wants your email, a password, and a confirmation click before you can do anything.

Attachments that break

Email and chat apps strip styling, block scripts, or refuse HTML outright. You end up sending a ZIP or a screenshot.

🔄

Tedious round-trips

Save file → open browser → log in → upload → copy link. It's 2026. This should take two seconds.

💬

Lost context

Pasting raw code into chat loses all formatting. The person on the other end has to save and open it themselves.

What makes it different

⚧
No sign-up, period.

No email, no password, no “check your inbox to confirm.” The site works the moment you open it.

⏱️
Links self-destruct.

Every link expires after 60 minutes by default. Nothing lives forever unless you choose to keep it. Perfect for drafts and previews.

🔒
Private by design.

URLs use random, unguessable strings. Nobody can browse or discover your content. Only people you give the link to can see it.

⚡
Fast everywhere.

Content is served from the global edge — it loads quickly whether you're in San Francisco, London, or Tokyo.
