## Design Context

The source of truth for all visual and content design is `DESIGN.md` at the repository root. Read it before designing or building any UI. The summary below is only orientation.

### Users
Everyone in a company who produces something worth keeping: engineers (markdown docs, RFCs, mockups) and business people (analyses, tables). Artfct is not a developer tool. Nothing on a marketing surface may require technical knowledge, and no terminal, CLI snippet, JSON, DOM selector or code block belongs on the landing page.

### Product idea
What a person's AI makes is shared to their team (the user chooses what to share), stored and indexed, and every AI tool on the team can read it with sources. It works across Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Cursor and other major AI tools.

### Aesthetic direction
Warm, editorial and calm: **Oxblood & Bone**. Bone ground `#F7F5F2`, Paper `#FBFAF8`, Hairline `#DEDAD2`, Charcoal `#262624`, one Oxblood accent `#701A24`. Newsreader serif for headlines, Manrope for interface and body. Hairline dividers over boxed cards, subtle motion that explains the product, a single light theme.

### Not this
The previous Solarized Light / ASCII / retrofuturistic direction was for the free single-file tool. It is being replaced. Do not reintroduce ASCII art, Solarized colors, Instrument Sans, gradients, or terminal imagery in new work. The currently deployed free tool may still show the old look until it is redesigned.

### Free tool behavior (unchanged)
Free links are `secure` and `ephemeral` by default, with no tier selection on the free flow.

### Copy rules
Plain language, sentence case, no invented customers, certifications, prices or statistics, and no jargon such as "harness" or "MCP" in body copy.
