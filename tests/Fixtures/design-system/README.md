# Sample design system

## Voice

Plain, warm and direct. Say what the thing does in the first sentence.

- Do: "Shared with your team in one step."
- Don't: "Leverage our best-in-class sharing synergies!"

## Visual foundations

- Colours: ink `#262624` for text, paper `#fbfaf8` for grounds, accent `#701a24` for headings and one rule per page.
- Typography: Manrope for interface and body text; Newsreader for document headlines.
- Spacing: a paragraph gap of 0.6em; a page margin of 0.75in.
- Imagery: quiet, photographic or line-based; no gradients.
- Iconography: one-stroke line icons, 1.5px, in ink.

## Logo rules

- Keep clear space of one square on every side.
- Minimum width 96px on screen.
- Use the accent square and the ink wordmark on paper. Do not recolour the square.

## Worked examples

- On brand: "Your team's notes, one search away."
- Off brand: "The ultimate AI knowledge revolution!"
